# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and clear. Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, or knowingly disruptive behavior. Focus technical discussion on evidence, scope, and the project’s stated behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this standard or threatens the safety of contributors. Report concerns privately through a repository-maintainer channel, such as GitHub’s private reporting mechanism when enabled. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public issue.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and project-related interactions.
